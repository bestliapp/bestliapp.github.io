Who operates Bestli

Bestli is a product name used by Zsolt Báthori, an individual. It is not a separate company or legal entity and has no CVR number. Contact: bestli.app.team@outlook.com.

1. Controller and roles

I, Zsolt Báthori, operate Bestli and decide how personal data is used to run the platform, manage accounts, maintain public business profiles and reviews, provide support, keep the service secure and manage subscriptions. I am the controller for those activities.

When a business uses Bestli to store its customers’ booking details, contact information, visit history or notes, that business decides why it needs those records and is generally the controller for them. I process them on the business’s instructions to provide booking and management tools. For questions about a particular appointment or a business’s own records, contact that business first. Where I process personal data on a business’s behalf, the business and I must have a written data-processing agreement in place before that data is added to Bestli.

3. Public information

A business profile is intended to be discoverable. The business chooses which details to publish, such as its name, location, services, hours, photos and reviews. Anyone using Discover or a shared profile link may see them. Booking contact details are shared with the specific business handling that booking; they are not part of a public profile.

A business’s staff members who have a Bestli account see the bookings made with them, including the client’s name, phone number and notes.

Staff members can also read the notes the business keeps about the clients they serve.

4. Service providers and other recipients

  • Supabase provides authentication, database and file-storage services.
  • Google provides Sign in with Google and, in the Android app, Google Play Billing for business subscriptions, Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications and Google Maps for map and address features.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and, in the iPhone and iPad app, the App Store and In-App Purchase for business subscriptions, push notifications (Apple Push Notification service) and Apple Maps for map and address features.
  • Microsoft Outlook provides the support email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this public information website and may process basic technical request information as part of hosting.
  • A business receives the booking and client information it needs to handle appointments made with it. Public profile and review information is visible as described above.
  • Brevo sends account e-mails, such as password resets.
  • Nager.Date provides public holiday dates when a business adds them; the request contains only the country and the year, plus the device’s IP address.
  • ntfy delivers an internal alert to the operator when feedback or a report arrives; the alert contains no personal data.
  • If a business owner or staff member subscribes to their bookings in a calendar app, that calendar provider receives those bookings (client names, phone numbers and services) under its own terms.

I do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Service providers may process information under their own terms and, where applicable, written data-processing arrangements.

Contact me for details about the safeguards applicable to your data.

6. How long information is kept

Account information is kept while the account is active. You can start account deletion in the app’s Settings. Deletion removes account and business information from the active service; limited copies may remain temporarily in provider backups or security records until their normal retention cycle expires. I may keep specific records as long as needed to meet a legal obligation, resolve a dispute or protect the service. A business may retain booking information it controls for its own lawful records after your Bestli account is deleted.

To show your information without a network connection, the app keeps a copy of what it last loaded on your device. This copy is excluded from device backups and is deleted when you sign out.

When you delete your account, your upcoming bookings are cancelled and the businesses are notified; past bookings stay with them as described above. When a business is deleted, clients with upcoming bookings there are notified that they are cancelled. Past days off are removed automatically.

A staff member leaves the team before deleting their account, so the business is told. Changes to the amount paid for a booking are kept in a log with the booking.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit or remove your profile, services and photos in Settings, delete your account, and turn off location access or notifications in your device settings. Depending on applicable law and circumstances, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Contact me by email to exercise these rights. If I process information for a business, I may need to direct the request to that business and help it respond.

You may complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work. In Denmark, that is the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

8. Security

Information sent between the app and its providers is protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS. I use provider access controls and take reasonable steps to limit access to what is needed to operate Bestli. No online service can promise absolute security.

9. Children

Bestli is not intended for anyone under 16, and the Terms require users to be at least 16. If you believe a person under 16 has created an account, contact me so I can review and remove it where appropriate.

10. Website and changes

This static information website currently has no advertising or analytics tags in its page code. Its host may still handle technical connection data. I may update this policy when the app, providers or legal requirements change; the date above shows the latest revision.
