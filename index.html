For businesses

Everything it takes to run the day.

Not only a calendar: rules, clients, staff and money live in the same place, so nothing has to be copied between apps.

Bookings & calendarServices & scheduleRules & policiesClientsStaff & teamMoneyInsightsGet foundNotificationsSecurity & your data

Bookings & calendar

  • Requests you confirm, or automatic confirmation per staff member
  • Dashboard with today's timeline, plus list, calendar and archive views
  • Add bookings yourself, with a warning when times overlap
  • Completed, no-show and cancelled are final, so the records stay reliable
  • Unanswered requests close on their own at their start time
  • Bookings in your phone's calendar, or a private feed for Apple or Google Calendar

Services & schedule

  • Services with price, duration, description and photo; packages that combine them
  • Clients can book several services in one go
  • Online, in person or both, with a meeting link sent once you confirm
  • Hours per weekday, lunch break, days off and special hours on single dates
  • Public holidays for your country added in one tap
  • Several clients per time slot for classes and groups; an optional break after a service

Rules & policies

  • A free-cancellation window you set, up to 72 hours
  • A late-cancellation and no-show fee shown to clients before they book
  • House rules clients accept on their first booking
  • The payment methods you accept, from cash and card to MobilePay, Swish, Vipps, BLIK, Pix and more
  • Block a client from booking; pause all new bookings while you're away

Clients

  • Every client with their booking history, notes and no-show count
  • Book a client again straight from their card
  • Reply to reviews, or hide one from public view
  • A rebooking reminder per service, sent to clients after a set number of weeks

Staff & team

  • Staff or chairs, each with their own weekly hours and days off
  • Invite staff by e-mail; they sign in with their own Bestli account
  • Staff see only their own bookings, with the client's notes and earlier visits
  • Pay arrangement per person: paid by the business, commission, or monthly chair rent
  • Turnover and earnings visible to both sides, by week, month or year

Money

  • Income and expenses, VAT owed and reclaimed, deductible and private entries
  • Completed bookings become income automatically, at the amount actually paid
  • Net profit by day, week, month, quarter, half year or year
  • A monthly revenue goal
  • CSV export for your bookkeeping

Insights

  • No-show rate
  • Popular services and popular times
  • New versus returning clients
  • By month, three months, year or all time, plus a summary on the 1st of each month

Get found

  • A listing in Discover, found by name, service, category, distance and more
  • Your own booking link and QR code, and a printable poster
  • Announce a last-minute free slot to clients who saved your business
  • Reviews with photos on your page

Notifications

  • New requests and changes from clients
  • A nudge when requests have waited over 3 hours
  • Tomorrow's bookings each evening, for you and each staff member
  • Team updates, such as a staff member leaving the team

Security & your data

  • Face ID, Touch ID or a PIN in front of the business tools
  • Export your bookings and revenue as CSV at any time
  • Delete the business or your account from the app
  • The last loaded data stays readable offline

FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find what you need? Read the guide or write to us.

What kinds of businesses can use Bestli?

Any business that works by appointment: beauty and hair, health and physiotherapy, therapy, sports and coaching, tutoring and languages, music, photography, events, cleaning, repairs, childcare, pet care, car services, tech and more — 29 categories in all. Services can be in person, online or both, and a time slot can take one client or a group.

Is Bestli free for clients?

Yes. Finding businesses, booking, managing your appointments and leaving reviews are free. Only businesses pay a subscription.

What does the business subscription include?

Every business tool for one business: dashboard, bookings and calendar, clients, revenue and insights, staff accounts, your booking link and QR code, and booking notifications. Without a subscription the business tools stay locked and clients can't find or book your business.

How long is the free period?

Businesses created before 1 January 2027 use every feature free until then. After that, a new business gets one free month, at least 30 days. The app tells you 7 days and 1 day before your plan ends.

Who are founding members?

Businesses that join early lock in a lower price of $4.99 per month for their first year. Whether you qualify is shown in the app when you subscribe.

Does Bestli handle payments between me and my clients?

No. Clients pay the business directly, the way the business accepts. Your page shows which payment methods you take, and the late-cancellation fee, if you set one, is shown to clients but not charged by Bestli.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Can my staff use it too?

Yes. Invite them by e-mail and they sign in with their own Bestli account. They see only their own bookings and earnings, and you decide how they're paid: by the business, by commission, or as monthly chair rent.

Can I see my bookings in my own calendar?

Yes. Bookings can go into your phone's calendar, and you can subscribe to a private Bestli calendar feed from Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or any app that supports calendar subscriptions.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) and Android, with one account and the same data on all of them. New features sometimes reach iOS first. The app is available in 32 languages.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You can export your bookings and revenue as CSV at any time, and delete your business or your whole account from the app. Upcoming bookings are cancelled and the other side is notified. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
