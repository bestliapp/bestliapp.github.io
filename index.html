FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find what you need? Read the guide or write to us.

Is Bestli free for clients?

Yes. Finding businesses, booking, managing your appointments and leaving reviews are free. Only businesses pay a subscription.

What does the business subscription include?

Every business tool for one business: dashboard, bookings and calendar, clients, revenue and insights, staff accounts, your booking link and QR code, and booking notifications. Without a subscription the business tools stay locked and clients can't find or book your business.

How does the free month work?

Every new business gets its first month free with every feature unlocked. The monthly subscription only starts after that, and the app tells you before the trial ends.

Who are founding members?

Businesses that join early lock in a lower price of $4.99 per month for their first year. Whether you qualify is shown in the app when you sign up.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later) and Android phones and tablets. The app is available in 32 languages.

Can I see my bookings in my own calendar?

Yes. Subscribe to your Bestli bookings from Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or any calendar app that supports calendar subscriptions, and they stay in sync.
