Getting started with Bestli

Set up your business in a few minutes and start taking bookings. Bestli works for any service you book by time – haircuts and massages, lessons and coaching, training sessions, photo shoots, repairs, cleaning or consultations.

  1. Create your business

    • Download Bestli and sign in with Apple, Google or e-mail.
    • Tap Settings → Start offering services on Bestli. A short setup walks you through your name, category, address, services and opening hours.
    • When you finish, your business appears in Discover and on the map, and clients can book you.
    Your business gets a free trial with every feature included.

  2. Add your services

    • Settings → Services & packages: name, price, duration, a short description and a photo for each service.
    • Optional per service: a break after it (cleaning, preparation), and a reminder for clients to book again after a few weeks.
    • Packages bundle services that are often booked together, so a client picks them with one tap.

  3. Set your hours

    • Settings → Hours & days off: hours for each weekday and a lunch break.
    • Days off as date ranges, and your country's public holidays with one tap.
    • Special hours on single dates – longer before a holiday, a half day, or a day you're normally closed.
    Clients only see times when you're open and free, so double bookings can't happen.

  4. Choose your booking rules

    • Settings → Booking rules: how long before the appointment clients can still cancel for free, and an optional fee you charge for late cancellations or no-shows.
    • Automatic confirmation, so bookings don't wait for you to accept them.
    • House rules that new clients accept before their first booking, and Multibook for classes or several chairs.

  5. Add your team

    • In Booking rules, add each staff member or chair. Clients can pick who they'd like, or leave it to you.
    • Give each person their own working days, hours and days off.
    • Invite them by e-mail: with their own Bestli account they see and manage their own bookings and earnings.
    • Set the money arrangement – paid by the business, a commission, or a monthly rent – and both of you see the same figures.

  6. Share your booking page

    • Settings → Reach clients: copy your booking link for your website, Instagram or Google profile.
    • Show your QR code, or print a poster or small cards for your counter and door.
    • Announce a free slot to clients who saved your business, and subscribe to your bookings in your phone's calendar.

  7. Your everyday work

    • The Dashboard shows today's timeline, what's next and the requests waiting for you.
    • In Bookings, confirm requests, mark appointments as completed or no-show, and add phone or walk-in bookings yourself.
    • Clients get a reminder the evening before; you get a summary of tomorrow and a monthly summary.

  8. Keep track of your money

    • Revenue from completed bookings is recorded for you; add your own income and expenses.
    • See your VAT, profit and progress towards a monthly goal, and export everything as CSV for your bookkeeping.
