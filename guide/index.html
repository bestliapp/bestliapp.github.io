How to use Bestli

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This guide walks you through Bestli step by step: first as a client booking an appointment, then as a business taking bookings. Everything works the same on iPhone, iPad and Android.

For clients

Get started

Download Bestli and sign in with Google, Apple or your e-mail address. As a client you never pay anything to use Bestli.

  • The app starts in your phone's language; change it any time in Settings → Preferences → Language.
  • Add your full name and phone number once in Settings → My details — they're filled in for every booking.

Find a business

Discover lists the businesses on Bestli. Type in the search field to find a business by name, service, category, city or country — for example “haircut”, “massage Vejle” or “online coaching”.

  • Tap the filter button next to the search field to narrow the list: category, open now, distance, online or in person, payment methods, rating and price level. Active filters appear as chips under the search field; tap ✕ to remove one.
  • Sort by rating, price, name or nearby (the app asks for your location once).
  • The Map tab shows the same businesses as pins.
  • Tap ♥ to save a business. Saved businesses can tell you about last-minute free slots.

Book an appointment

Open a business to see its services, prices, opening hours, address, payment methods, reviews and cancellation policy. Tap Book now:

  1. Choose one or more services — durations and prices add up automatically.
  2. Pick a date and a free time. Taken times are shown greyed out; tap one to join the waitlist.
  3. If the business has staff, choose who you'd like under the time, or leave it on No preference. People who are busy or off then are crossed out.
  4. Read and accept the house rules if the business has any, add a note if you like, and tap Book.

Most businesses confirm requests by hand — you'll get a notification. Some confirm automatically, and your booking is confirmed straight away. Want the same time every week? Switch on Repeat.

Your bookings

My bookings shows what's coming up and your history. Tap a booking to:

  • add it to your calendar, get directions, call the business, or join the online meeting (shown once the booking is confirmed);
  • reschedule or cancel — until the business's free-cancellation time. After that, call the business. Some businesses charge a fee for late cancellations or no-shows; it's always shown before you book;
  • see how you can pay. Bestli never takes payment for appointments — you pay the business directly.

The evening before an appointment you get a reminder. After the visit you can rate it and add a photo; the business can reply.

For businesses

Set up your business

In Settings, tap Start offering services on Bestli. A short wizard asks for your business name and category, country, address and phone, whether you work in person, online or both, your hours, your currency, payment methods and services, and photos. Everything can be changed later.

Your business settings live under Settings → Your business: Profile, Services & packages, Hours & days off, Booking rules and Money & tax. Your first month is free.

Services and packages

  • Each service has a name, price, duration, optional description and photo.
  • Break after this service keeps time free after it for cleaning or preparation; clients can't book into it.
  • Rebooking reminder sends the client a “time to book again?” notification a set number of weeks after their visit.
  • If you work both online and in person, mark each service as in person, online or both.
  • Packages group services that are often booked together, so a client can pick them in one tap.

Hours and days off

  • Set your hours with one row per weekday and a switch for closed days. Apply to all open days copies one day's hours.
  • Add a lunch break if you take one.
  • Special hours change a single date — a half day, longer hours before a holiday, or opening on a day you're normally closed.
  • Days off are added as from–to ranges. Add public holidays fills in your country's holidays for this year and next.

Booking rules

  • Staff / chairs: add the people or chairs clients can choose. Tap a name to give that person their own working hours and days off, and to invite them to their own Bestli account (see Your team).
  • Automatic confirmation: requests are confirmed as soon as they arrive, per staff member.
  • Multibook: let several clients book the same time, for classes or parallel chairs.
  • House rules: a text every client accepts before their first booking (and again when you change it).
  • Cancellation: until how many hours before the appointment clients can cancel or reschedule in the app, and an optional late-cancellation / no-show fee in %. Bestli only shows the fee; you charge it yourself.

Handle bookings

New requests appear on the Dashboard and in Bookings, and you get a notification. Confirm or decline them there.

  • After the appointment, mark it completed or no-show. These are final, so the app asks once more. A completed booking adds its income to Revenue automatically.
  • If the client paid a different amount, use Edit amount paid on the completed booking — every change is logged.
  • You can add bookings yourself with + (walk-ins or phone bookings), keep notes on clients, and see a client's earlier visits and no-shows.
  • Each evening you get a summary of tomorrow's bookings.

Your team

Staff can have their own Bestli account. In Booking rules, tap a staff member, enter their e-mail and tap Send invitation. They accept in their own app's Settings and switch to the team view, where they see only their own bookings (My schedule) and their Earnings.

  • Choose the arrangement: paid by the business, a commission to the business in %, or a monthly rent. Both of you see the same figures.
  • Removing someone keeps their read-only access for 7 days so they can export their records. If they still have bookings, you can move them to a colleague or cancel them.
  • A team member can leave the team in their Settings; they have to leave before deleting their account.

Reach clients

Under Settings → Reach clients:

  • Share your business sends your Bestli link; it opens your page in the app or in a browser.
  • Show QR code — and Print a poster for your counter or window, or cut-out cards.
  • Announce a free slot notifies clients who saved your business about a last-minute opening (up to 3 a day).
  • Subscribe in Calendar shows your bookings in your phone's calendar app. Keep that link private.

Revenue and records

Revenue shows income and expenses by day, week, month or year, with VAT if you set a rate in Money & tax, and progress toward your monthly goal. Add expenses with +, and export to CSV for your bookkeeping. Settings → Account & data → Export my data downloads all your bookings and records.

Subscription, pause and closing

  • Every new business gets one month free. After that, subscribe in Settings → Subscription & Billing (through the App Store or Google Play). Clients always use Bestli for free.
  • Pause bookings hides your business from Discover and stops new bookings, for example while you're on holiday. Existing bookings stay.
  • Delete business (Settings → Account & data) removes the business but keeps your account as a client account. Cancel your subscription first. With a team, your team gets 7 days' notice first.
