How to use Bestli

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Bestli works the same on iPhone, iPad and Android. Pick your path below — booking as a client, or taking bookings as a business.

For clients

1Get started

  • Download Bestli and sign in with Google, Apple or your e-mail address. Bestli is always free for clients.
  • Change the language in Settings → Preferences → Language.
  • Save your name and phone number in Settings → My details — they're filled in for every booking.
↑ Back to top

2Find a business

  • In Discover, search by name, service, category, city or country.
  • Tap the filter button next to the search field to narrow the list: category, open now, distance, online or in person, payment methods, rating and price.
  • Map shows the same businesses as pins.
  • Tap ♥ to save a business — saved businesses can tell you about last-minute free slots.
↑ Back to top

3Book an appointment

  1. Open a business and tap Book Now.
  2. Choose one or more services, then a date and a free time. Tap a taken time to join the waitlist.
  3. Pick a staff member under the time, or keep No preference.
  4. Accept the house rules if asked and tap Book. Switch on Repeat for a regular appointment.

Most businesses confirm requests by hand and you get a notification; some confirm them automatically.

↑ Back to top

4Your bookings

  • In My Bookings, tap a booking to add it to your calendar, get directions, call the business or join an online meeting.
  • Reschedule or cancel until the business's free-cancellation time — after that, call them. A late-cancellation or no-show fee is always shown before you book.
  • You pay the business directly. Bestli never takes payment for appointments.
  • You get a reminder the evening before, and can rate the visit afterwards.
↑ Back to top
For businesses

1Set up your business

  • In Settings, tap Start offering services on Bestli and follow the short setup.
  • Everything can be changed later in Settings → Your business.
  • Every new business gets its first month free.
↑ Back to top

2Services & packages

  • Give each service a name, price, duration, and optionally a description and photo.
  • A break after a service keeps time free for cleaning or preparation.
  • A rebooking reminder invites the client back after a set number of weeks.
  • Packages bundle services that are often booked together.
↑ Back to top

3Hours & days off

  • Set one row per weekday and switch off the days you're closed. Add a lunch break if you take one.
  • Special hours change a single date — even a day you're normally closed.
  • Add days off as from–to ranges. Add public holidays fills in your country's holidays.
↑ Back to top

4Booking rules

  • Add the staff or chairs clients can choose. Tap a name to set their own hours and days off, or invite them to their own account.
  • Choose automatic confirmation, Multibook (several clients at the same time) and house rules.
  • Under cancellation, set until when clients can cancel in the app, and an optional late-cancellation / no-show fee.
↑ Back to top

5Handle bookings

  • New requests appear on the Dashboard and in Bookings, with a notification. Confirm or decline them there.
  • After the visit, mark it completed or no-show — this is final. Completed bookings add their income to Revenue.
  • Paid a different amount? Use Edit amount paid. Add walk-ins and phone bookings with +.
↑ Back to top

6Your team

  • In Booking rules, tap a staff member, enter their e-mail and tap Send invitation.
  • Once they accept, they see only their own bookings in My schedule, and their Earnings.
  • Set the arrangement: paid by the business, a commission, or a monthly rent.
  • Removed members keep read-only access for 7 days so they can export their records.
↑ Back to top

7Reach clients

  • Share your business or Show QR code — and print a poster or cards for your counter.
  • Announce a free slot tells clients who saved you about a last-minute opening.
  • Subscribe in Calendar shows your bookings in your phone's calendar.
↑ Back to top

8Revenue

  • See income and expenses by day, week, month or year, with VAT and your monthly goal set in Money & tax.
  • Export CSV files for your bookkeeping; Export my data downloads everything.
↑ Back to top

9Subscription, pause and closing

  • After the free month, subscribe in Subscription & Billing. Clients always use Bestli for free.
  • Pause bookings hides your business from Discover, for example while you're on holiday.
  • Delete business removes the business but keeps your account. Cancel your subscription first.
↑ Back to top

10Need help?

  • Tap 💬 at the top of Settings to send feedback or report a problem — you can attach a screenshot.
  • Or e-mail bestli.app.team@outlook.com. We read every message.
