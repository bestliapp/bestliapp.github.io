Delete your Bestli account

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Bestli - Book Appointments account (developer: Zsolt Báthori) and what happens to your data.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Bestli and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Business owners: tap Export my data first if you need your bookings and revenue records for your bookkeeping.
  4. Tap Delete account, type DELETE to confirm, and tap Delete account again.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email bestli.app.team@outlook.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Bestli account”. I delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email address, password or Google/Apple sign-in link).
  • Your preferences, favourites and the device's push-notification token.
  • If you own a business: the business profile, services, photos, staff, opening hours, all of its bookings, revenue entries and client notes.

What is kept

  • Bookings you made as a client at other businesses stay with those businesses for their own records, but are no longer linked to your account.
  • Reviews you wrote stay on the business's page without being linked to your account.
  • Limited copies may remain in our providers' backups or security logs until their normal retention cycle ends (typically up to 30 days).
  • A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play is managed by Apple or Google; deleting your account does not cancel it there — cancel it in your Apple Account or Google Play subscriptions.

See the full Privacy Policy for details.
